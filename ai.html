As artificial intelligence (AI) evolves beyond searching for information and providing answers to AI agents that directly perform tasks, the method of semiconductor research and development is also changing. It is an attempt to increase research and development efficiency by automating complex design, simulation, and verification processes. TERA Lab, led by Professor Kim Jeong-ho of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at KAIST, will hold an online event on the 11th.
