[충청뉴스 이성현 기자] 국립한밭대학교 연구진이 건축설비시스템을 통해 학술적 성과를 인정받았다.이와 함께 인공지능(AI) 등 첨단 과학기술 분야에서도 연이어 성과를 거두며 대학의 연구 경쟁력을 유감없이 입증했다.조진균 교수 연구실, 데이터센터 냉각시스템 성능 분석 우수논문상 수상한밭대는 건축설비시스템공학과 조진균 교수가 지도하는 ESDL 연구실의 안지혁 석사과정생이 ‘2026년 대한건축학회 추계학술발표대회’에서 우수발표논문상 대표 수상의 영예를 안았다고 23일 밝혔다.이번 대회는 지난 21일부터 23일까지 대전컨벤션센터에서 개최됐으

[Chungcheong News Reporter Seong-Hyun Lee] Researchers at Hanbat National University were recognized for their academic achievements through architectural facility systems. In addition, they have achieved successive achievements in cutting-edge science and technology fields such as artificial intelligence (AI), proving the university's research competitiveness. Professor Jin-Kyun Cho's laboratory received the Outstanding Paper Award for Data Center Cooling System Performance Analysis. Batdae, a master's student at ESDL Lab led by Professor Jin-Kyun Cho of the Department of Architectural Facilities and Systems Engineering, is a master's student at the ESDL Lab led by Professor Jin-Kyun Cho of the Architectural Institute of Korea in 2026. It was announced on the 23rd that it received the honor of winning the Best Presentation Paper Award at the ‘Fall Academic Presentation Contest’. This contest was held at the Daejeon Convention Center from the 21st to the 23rd.
