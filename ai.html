LG Uplus said on the 29th that two related papers were selected for the on-device intelligence workshop of ’Neurips 2026’, one of the world's three AI societies. LG Uplus has studied how to efficiently execute and learn small language models (sLM) even with limited memory and power by applying token optimization technology to smartphone environments.
