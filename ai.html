In a paper conducted as a national research and development (R&D) project, errors were found in 17 out of 40 references and the paper was withdrawn, but the National Research Foundation of Korea was found to have failed to filter them out during the publication process. At the National Assembly's Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee's audit of science and technology research institutions, including the National Research Foundation of Korea, held at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology in Daejeon on the 20th, Rep. Lee Joo-hee of the Democratic Party of Korea asked Hong Won-hwa, chairman of the National Research Foundation of Korea, to strengthen the research performance verification system, saying, "It has happened that no one checks papers and references conducted as national projects until they are published in external papers."
