According to Nexon on the 9th, researchers Ji-seok Ji, Yeon-woo Jeong, Sang-ho Hwang, and Seong-il Ryu from the Applied Analysis Department of the AI ​​Headquarters analyzed about 19.37 million Steam user reviews. This is a study that looked at situations in which AI is welcomed and rejected in games. Nexon explained that as a result of the analysis, there is a positive response to AI that helps users or blends in naturally with the play process. Conversely, it only plays a secondary role in the game or is difficult for the user to control.
