Seoul National University researchers have developed a technology that allows humanoid robots to continuously perform multiple tasks by integrating separately learned walking and object manipulation data into one artificial intelligence (AI) system. It is attracting attention as a technology that will increase humanoid learning efficiency as it can perform complex tasks without separately collecting continuous demonstration data that switches between movement and manipulation. The research team of Professor Seonghoe Oh of the Department of Electrical and Information Engineering at Seoul National University College of Engineering is developing a vision, language, and action (VLA) framework that collects gait and object manipulation data independently and learns them together.
