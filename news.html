The number of immigrant visas issued to Koreans has also decreased by 19% compared to the monthly average of a year or so ago, and the issuance of visas for international students and foreign workers is also showing a decline. According to reports from Seoul Economic Daily and SBS on the 19th, the number of immigrant visas issued by the U.S. State Department in February 2026 was about 26,000, which is only half of the monthly average of about 52,000 before President Trump took office.
